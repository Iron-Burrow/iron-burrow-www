Positioning

Not another dashboard. Not a generic data provider.

Iron Burrow is built for cases where the answer needs to be traceable, reproducible, and useful for compliance workflows.

Instead of hiding operational details behind screenshots or opaque vendor output, the product moves toward source-aware evidence that can be inspected by technical and compliance teams.

Why it exists

Compliance review should be inspectable.

Crypto compliance work often depends on fragmented data, manual review, screenshots, RPC calls, spreadsheets, and vendor black boxes.

Iron Burrow exists to make that process more inspectable, with clearer answers about source, freshness, reproducibility, and operational suitability.

Evidence questions

Every result should make review easier.

Where did this data come from?
Can we reproduce it?
Is it fresh enough to trust?
Is it suitable for a compliance or operational decision?
