Iron Burrow is built by José María Sosa, a backend engineer focused on crypto infrastructure, Rust systems, deterministic data pipelines, and source-aware on-chain intelligence.

The project is currently founder-led and in private Beta. Its early focus is narrow by design: make on-chain evidence easier to inspect, reproduce, and use inside compliance and operational workflows.

José's work centers on practical infrastructure problems where correctness, traceability, and boring reliability matter more than surface area.
